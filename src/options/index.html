Slop Flag settings

What gets sent. In Auto mode, the text of every page that qualifies (YouTube watch pages, and pages that look like articles, on sites not in the never-check list) is sent to the selected provider as soon as it loads. In Manual mode, text is sent only when you press “Check this page” in the popup. The Heuristic provider runs offline and sends nothing.

Accuracy. Jev is a general-purpose text classifier, not a trained AI-text detector. Its accuracy for detecting AI-written text is unverified, and its API contract was taken from third-party write-ups. Treat flags as hints, not proof.

“Test provider” sends a fixed sample paragraph using the saved settings and shows the raw response.
